emberwave

Plays PyroWave streams (.pwv1), decoded with WebGPU in this page by emberwave. Nothing is uploaded. Without a file, it plays a demo that the page draws and encodes first.

As packets, each frame is cut at its blocks into packets as a host sends them (a block larger than a packet goes alone), shuffled and some dropped, as a network may, and the player decodes what arrives. A frame of which the coarsest level and more than the chosen share of the blocks arrived shows with the lost finer blocks blank, and other frames are skipped; a lost packet costs no other frame.
